Bassio UI Brand Guide

Design system applied to the Player Profile · Stadium Dashboard build. Fonts, palette, gradients, components and usage rules.

Navy on white for documents and light surfaces · White on navy for the app header band · Gold version for premium / B-Prime contexts. Minimum height 32 px in the UI; clear space equal to the height of the "b" bowl on all sides.

Rule

Anton is reserved for headlines, menu labels and big numbers; it is never used for running text or form fields. Montserrat carries everything else. Uppercase Anton gets 2–8% letter-spacing; Montserrat labels in caps get 8–12%.

5 · Layout rules

01

Band → menu → panels. The navy band holds identity (photo 4:5, name, highlights, SoS meter). The main menu overlaps the band by 52 px and stays sticky. Everything below swaps in place; no page navigation.

02

Grid. Max width 1040 px. One column under 860 px, two columns above. Cards use 16 px radius, 1 px line border, 16 px padding, 12 px internal gap.

03

Private vs public. Public view removes Wallet and Settings, removes Badges card and ad slider from Overview, adds the Active Membership card after demographics, and adds a Badges tab with endorse counters.

04

Pop-ups. Bottom sheet on phones, centred dialog from 700 px. Used for tournament history, wallet histories, top-up, transfer, loyalty tiers and confirmations.

05

Locked fields. After admin verification, Birthdate, ID/Passport number and Nationality are read-only and carry a gold lock label.
